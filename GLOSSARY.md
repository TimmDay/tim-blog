# tim-blog

A personal blog about AI progress and its effect on software engineering, society and engineering teams. It also serves as a hiring portfolio.

## Language

### Content

**Post**:
A single piece of writing on the site, which may contain images and Widgets. A Post is either a Draft or published.
_Avoid_: Article, entry, blog

**Draft**:
A Post that isn't published yet. It shows only in previews, never on the live site.
_Avoid_: Unpublished post, WIP

**Idea**:
A rough note in Tim's Obsidian vault that may one day become a Post. Importing it turns it into a Draft.
_Avoid_: Draft (until imported), note, stub

**Widget**:
An interactive element embedded in a Post, such as a flashcard, an accordion or a time-slider map.
_Avoid_: Component, embed, island, interactive

**Fallback**:
The static version of a Widget (image, table or text summary) that conveys its point without interaction.
_Avoid_: Placeholder, no-JS version

**Tag**:
A free-form label on a Post. Posts may have several.
_Avoid_: Topic, category

### Hiring

**CV**:
A downloadable résumé offered on the Contact page, with private details removed. There is one CV per CV Variant.
_Avoid_: Resume, résumé

**CV Variant**:
A version of the CV aimed at one kind of role: Engineering Manager or AI Consultant.
_Avoid_: CV type, version, flavour
